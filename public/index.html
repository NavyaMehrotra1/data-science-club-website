01 / Mission

Learn the tools. Then build with them.

The Data Science Club is JHU's student-led, project-based home for data science. Members learn relevant datasets, libraries, and machine learning methods, then apply them in team projects. Any Johns Hopkins student can join, from any major and any year. No prior experience is required.

  1. 01

    Data Science 101

    At the start of each semester, beginner-friendly workshops cover the basics of data science and machine learning, including practical skills such as Python, data analysis, and machine learning.

  2. 02

    Themed projects

    Each semester, members build around a theme. Themes include data science in medicine and data science in finance. A theme runs for about a month, and members can keep a project going after that. Project ideas include medical image classification, predicting disease risk, fraud detection, and stock price movement prediction.

  3. 03

    Workshops and data labs

    Kickoff workshops introduce the tools for a theme and help members choose projects and teams. Workshops are guided and include in-class exercises. Data labs are general body meetings where members collaborate on those projects.
